Sonic X Shadow Generations

Released in October 2024, this bundle pairs a remaster of Sonic Generations with a brand-new campaign starring Shadow the Hedgehog.

Sonic Generations

The 2011 anniversary game returns with Classic and Modern Sonic racing through stages from across his history.

Shadow's Campaign

Shadow explores a mysterious white space and faces Black Doom in a story built around his past.

New Powers

Shadow uses Chaos Control and Doom Powers to slow time, cross gaps, and fight in new ways.

Why It Is Great

  1. Smooth, modern 3D speed
  2. Two games' worth of content in one package
  3. A fresh, stylish take on Shadow's abilities
