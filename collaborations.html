Project '06

Project '06 is a free, fan-made reimagining of Sonic the Hedgehog (2006), created by community members rather than SEGA.

A Second Chance

The 2006 original had big ideas but was held back by bugs and long loading times. Fans set out to fix that.

Community Made

Passionate players volunteer their time and skills, showing how a fanbase can shape a series.

Better Feel

The goal is smoother controls and a more polished experience while keeping the spirit of the story and its characters.

Why It Is Great

  • Proves a rough game can have a loyal, creative fanbase
  • Free to follow as a community project
  • Unofficial, so details may change as the team works
