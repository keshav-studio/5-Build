Sonic Mania

Released in 2017, Sonic Mania is a love letter to the classic 2D Sonic games, built with fast pixel-art platforming and a lot of heart.

Who Made It

Christian Whitehead, Headcannon, and PagodaWest Games developed the game with SEGA, and all three were longtime Sonic fans.

Remixed Classics

Familiar zones like Green Hill and Chemical Plant return with new layouts, new bosses, and surprising twists.

Sights and Sounds

Sharp sprites and a lively soundtrack by Tee Lopes capture the 16-bit feeling without feeling stuck in the past.

Why It Is Great

  • Play as Sonic, Tails, or Knuckles
  • Brand-new zones like Studiopolis and Press Garden
  • Sonic Mania Plus added Mighty, Ray, and Encore Mode
